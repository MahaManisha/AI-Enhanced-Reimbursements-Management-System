How Our System Works

A streamlined five-step process from claim submission to automated risk analysis and financial review.

01

Submit Reimbursement Claim

Employees log in, enter expense details, select a category, and upload supporting bills or receipts.

02

Machine Learning Prediction

The Random Forest model analyzes the expense amount, category, bill availability, and historical employee reimbursement patterns to predict:

Likely Approved Requires Review High Rejection Risk
03

Generative AI Audit

The LLM generates reimbursement summaries, expense observations, missing-documentation alerts, and review suggestions.

04

Finance Team Review

The finance administrator reviews the submitted claim, supporting bills, ML prediction, and AI-generated observations before approving or rejecting it.

05

Track Reimbursement Status

Employees can monitor their claim status, view decision comments, and access their reimbursement history through the employee dashboard.
